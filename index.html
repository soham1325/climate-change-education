Introduction to Climate Change

Climate change refers to long-term changes in temperature, rainfall, and weather patterns. Human activities such as burning fossil fuels, deforestation, and industrialization are the major contributors.

Major Causes

Fossil Fuels

Coal, oil, and gas release greenhouse gases.

Deforestation

Reduces carbon absorption.

Industries

Emit pollutants and CO₂.

Effects of Climate Change

Rising Temperature

Increase in global average temperature.

Sea Level Rise

Melting glaciers raise sea levels.

Extreme Weather

Floods, droughts, cyclones.
